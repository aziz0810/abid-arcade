How to play

There's a secret footballer. Guess any player and you'll see where they rank by similarity to the secret one, out of 7,000+ players. The secret player is #1. Guesses are unlimited.

  • Similarity comes from nationality, clubs played for, leagues, position and age. Players who were at the same club in the same seasons count as teammates and get a big bonus.
  • A low number means you're close. If Rivaldo ranks in the top 10, the answer is probably Brazilian and played for Barcelona or Milan.
  • The clue chips under each guess show which kind of link matched. Solid means an exact match, outlined means near (same continent, same area of the pitch, a few years apart in age). A red Teammate chip means they shared a dressing room. Switch chips off below for a harder game.
  • Stuck? Closer player gives you a name ranked about halfway between your best guess and the answer. The other hints reveal one fact each. Hints are counted in your result.
  • Daily match is the same star player for everyone each day. Unlimited deals a new player whenever you want, at the difficulty you choose.

Player data: Transfermarkt via the open transfermarkt-datasets project (snapshot July 2026), plus hand-entered careers for legends who retired before 2012.
