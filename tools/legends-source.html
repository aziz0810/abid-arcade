How to play

There's a secret footballer. Guess any player and you'll see where they rank by similarity to the secret one. The secret player is #1. Guesses are unlimited.

  • Similarity comes from nationality, clubs played for, leagues, position and age. Players who were likely teammates get a bonus.
  • A low number means you're close. If Rivaldo ranks #5, the answer is probably Brazilian and played for Barcelona or Milan.
  • The clue chips under each guess show which kind of link matched. A solid chip is an exact match; an outlined chip is a near match (same continent, same area of the pitch, within a few years of age). Switch them off below for a harder game.
  • Stuck? Closer player gives you a name ranked about halfway between your best guess and the answer. The other hints each reveal one fact. Hints are counted in your result.
  • Daily match is the same for everyone each day. Unlimited deals a new secret player whenever you like.
